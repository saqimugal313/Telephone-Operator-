📅 CERP SWM PROJECT · LAHORE 2026

The CERP SWM Project (Solid Waste Management Project) is a research-based field project conducted by the Centre for Economic Research in Pakistan (CERP) in collaboration with the Lahore Waste Management Company (LWMC).

The purpose of this project is to study and improve the waste management and recycling system in Lahore. Researchers collect information from households, waste collectors, recycling agents, and community members to identify problems and suggest better solutions for urban cleanliness and waste management.

Main Objectives of the Project
♻Improve waste collection systems across Lahore
♿Study recycling practices and their efficiency
👥Understand public behaviour regarding cleanliness
📋Improve coordination between public and private waste workers
📊Collect accurate field data for research and policy-making
🔎Support evidence-based decision-making for urban management
Field Activities in the Project
Household surveys
Interviews with community members
Observation of waste collection points
Monitoring waste management activities
Data collection using tablets / mobile apps
Reporting and documentation
Team coordination and supervision

Common Interview Questions About the Project
Q1 What is CERP?
Answer
CERP stands for Centre for Economic Research in Pakistan. It is a research organization that works on surveys, policy research, data analysis, and social development projects to improve public services in Pakistan.
Q2 What is the purpose of the SWM project?
Answer
The purpose is to improve waste collection, recycling systems, and urban cleanliness in Lahore through research and accurate field data collection. The findings help policymakers make better decisions about waste management.
Q3 What does LWMC stand for?
Answer
LWMC stands for Lahore Waste Management Company. It is the government body responsible for solid waste collection and cleanliness in Lahore, and it is the key partner in this research project.
Q4 Why is this project important?
Answer
This project helps improve public cleanliness, environmental conditions, recycling systems, and waste management policies in Lahore. It provides evidence that government and organizations can use to make better decisions for citizens.
Q5 What type of data will be collected in this project?
Answer
Data related to households, waste collection methods, recycling practices, public opinions on cleanliness, sanitation conditions, and overall waste management practices in different union councils of Lahore.
Q6 Why is accurate data important in this project?
Answer
Accurate data helps researchers and policymakers make better decisions and improve public services. If the data is wrong, the entire research and its recommendations become unreliable, wasting resources and failing communities.
Q7 What tools are used during field surveys?
Answer
Tablets, mobile applications, survey forms, and observation sheets are the main tools used during field surveys. Digital tools help ensure faster and more accurate data entry and submission.
Q8 Are field visits required for this project?
Answer
Yes. All field staff members must travel to different union councils and areas of Lahore to conduct surveys and collect data. The project is entirely field-based and requires physical presence in communities.
Q9 What qualities are most important for field staff?
Answer
The most important qualities are communication skills, honesty, teamwork, discipline, patience, and responsibility. Field staff represent CERP and LWMC in communities, so professional behaviour is essential at all times.
Q10 How should field staff behave with respondents?
Answer
Staff should always behave politely, respectfully, and professionally. They must introduce themselves clearly, explain the survey purpose, respect the respondent's time and privacy, and never pressure anyone to participate.
